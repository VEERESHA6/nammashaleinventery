📱 Namma-Shaale Inventory
📌 Overview

Namma-Shaale Inventory is an Android application designed to help schools manage and track their assets such as sports equipment, lab tools, and tablets. It enables teachers to monitor item conditions, report issues, and ensure proper utilization of resources.

❗ Problem Statement

Schools receive government-funded resources, but there is no efficient system to track their condition. Items often get damaged or lost without timely detection, leading to poor resource management and accountability issues.

🎯 Solution

This app acts as a Digital Asset Auditor that allows:

Easy asset registration
Regular condition monitoring
Issue reporting and repair tracking
Dashboard-based overview of all assets
🚀 Features
📦 Asset Register
Add items with name, serial number, and photo
🔍 Condition Tracking
Update item status (Working / Needs Repair / Broken)
📝 Issue Log
Record lost or damaged items with reason and date
🛠️ Repair Requests
Track items that require maintenance
📊 Dashboard
View total assets and repair status
